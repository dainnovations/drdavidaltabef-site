Product & Adoption Consulting

Will clinicians actually adopt the thing you built?

I help companies building clinician-facing software — EHRs, mental health tech — understand whether therapists will actually adopt their product. And why, or why not.

Why me

Five years as the go-to tech guy across multiple clinics. Multiple EHR rollouts — I've watched adoption succeed and fail up close, from inside the clinic. Deep evaluation of the market when I chose my own tools. I'm also a practicing clinician, so I don't have to guess how therapists think about your product. I am your user.

How I can help

  • Adoption reviews. A clinician's-eye walkthrough of your product: where trust builds, where it breaks, what gets abandoned after week one.
  • Workflow reality checks. How your product actually fits (or doesn't) into the day of a working therapist or clinic.
  • AI trust. Clinicians are a famously skeptical audience for AI features. I help you understand what earns their trust and what torches it.
