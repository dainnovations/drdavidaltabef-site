Altabef Advisory · AI & Behavioral Health

Will clinicians actually use the thing you're building?

I offer first-impression product audits for clinician-facing AI products. I go through your product the way a working clinician would, and I tell you what I see. I see a lot of products that lose clinicians in the first 15 seconds.

I'm a practicing psychologist, so I don't have to guess how clinicians think about your product. Before opening my own practice, I was the go-to tech guy across multiple clinics, through multiple EHR rollouts. I heard therapists' reactions, complaints, difficulties.

I also believe that there is tremendous potential right now for AI clinical products to do real good, to support clinicians and meaningfully improve patient outcomes.

How I can help

  • First-impression audit. I go through your product as your target clinician. I'll give you my impressions and a one-page brief with the highest leverage insights. Trust, implementation, onboarding.
  • AI trust. Many clinicians are skeptical about AI. Some of that comes from not knowing. I'll tell you how you can help clinicians understand the value of your product and be open to trying it.
