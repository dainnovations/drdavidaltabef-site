ADHD & Executive Functioning Coaching

Get unstuck. Stay grounded.

Stuck? Procrastinating an important thing? Overwhelmed? Feel like you're not quite reaching your potential?

Maybe talking a couple of things through with someone could be cool. Maybe it doesn't hurt if that person is a psychologist specializing in adult ADHD who has spent thousands of hours sitting with smart, high-achieving people with ADHD.

Wait a minute... this is gonna sound weird, but... you're on the website of someone who fits that exact description.

What sessions actually look like

Sometimes we talk big picture. Sometimes we target one specific thing you're stuck on — a task, a habit, some corner of your life — break it down, and work through it together in real time.

  • Walking phone calls. Lots of clients like to get outside and walk while we talk. It works.
  • Video + screen share. We look at your actual workflows and AI tools together and make them easier.
  • Do the thing, together. Toss on a hands-free headset and actually do the chore while we talk. You leave the call with it done.

What I bring

I blend my background in psychology — cognition, behavior change, executive functioning — with low-stress productivity and operational excellence tools to make stuff easier, not just organized. We use your own problem-solving skills plus everything I've learned from thousands of hours with smart adults with ADHD, and build simple, step-by-step, evidence-based systems that get you unstuck.

Plus: I'm a laid-back, nice person, a good listener, and easy to talk to. Because I'm not judgmental.

Coaching is not therapy

Coaching is practical, forward-looking, and skills-focused. If you're looking for clinical care — including the emotional side of ADHD — see my psychotherapy page. Happy to help you figure out which fits.
